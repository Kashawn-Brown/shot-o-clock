Privacy Policy — Shot O'Clock

Last updated: July 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KB Forge ("we", "us") handles information in the Shot O'Clock mobile app (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to this policy.

Shot O'Clock is designed to be played without an account. We collect as little as possible.

Who the App is for

Shot O'Clock is a social drinking-game app intended only for people who are of legal drinking age in their country or region. It is not directed to, and must not be used by, anyone under the legal drinking age.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Display name — a name you choose so others in your party can recognize you. It can be anything; it does not have to be your real name. It is visible to other members of a party you join.
  • Age and terms confirmations — that you confirmed you are of legal drinking age and accepted the terms.

Information created as you use the App

  • An anonymous identifier — a random ID assigned to your device so the App can keep track of your parties and roster status. It is not linked to your real identity, email, or phone number.
  • Party and game data — parties you create or join, join codes, your roster status (active / out / removed), round outcomes, and related timestamps.
  • Push notification token — if you enable notifications, a device token used to deliver party alerts. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.
  • Basic technical data — limited log/diagnostic data needed to operate and secure the service.

What we do NOT collect

  • No real name, email address, phone number, or password (there are no accounts).
  • No precise location.
  • No payment information.
  • No advertising identifiers, and we do not run ads or third-party ad tracking.

How we use information

  • To run the game — sync the countdown, keep the roster live, and record round results across everyone in a party.
  • To send party notifications you have opted into.
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the service, and to prevent abuse.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share information with (service providers)

We use a small number of service providers to run the App. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and backend hosting (stores your party and profile data). Data is hosted in the Canada (Central) region.
  • Expo, Apple (APNs), and Google (FCM) — delivery of push notifications to your device.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users or the service.

Retention and your choices

  • Display name / notifications — you can change your display name, and enable or disable notifications in your device settings, at any time.
  • Reset this device — the App lets you reset your device, which clears your local data and starts a fresh anonymous identity. Party data already recorded on the server may be retained as needed to operate the service.
  • We keep information for as long as needed to provide the service and for legitimate operational, legal, and security purposes.

Security

We use reasonable technical measures, including access controls that prevent people who are not members of a party from reading its data. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
